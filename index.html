A companion, not a replacement.

Keep the official Spotify experience and add a lightweight desktop layer for the controls and information you use most.

01

Global shortcuts

Put essential playback actions behind keyboard shortcuts so they remain available while you work.

02

Mini player

Keep track information and basic playback controls visible without leaving your current application.

03

Useful integrations

Connect supported services such as Last.fm and Discord Rich Presence when you want them.
